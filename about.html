01 — INTRODUCTION

What is PromptCraft?

Getting useful results from AI starts with giving it effective instructions. However, writing a good prompt can sometimes be challenging.

PromptCraft helps bridge that gap. It analyzes the instructions you provide and refines them while preserving your original intent, making your prompts clearer and more purposeful.
